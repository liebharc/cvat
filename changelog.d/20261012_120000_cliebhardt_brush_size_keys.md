### Added

- \[UI\] Shortcuts to change the brush/eraser size in the mask drawing
  toolbox, `+`/`-` by default. Holding a key keeps resizing the brush,
  with a step that grows with the brush size.

### Changed

- \[UI\] "Move to background" and "Move to foreground" do not have default
  shortcuts anymore, `+`/`-` are used to change the brush size instead.
